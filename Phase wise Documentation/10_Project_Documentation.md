## Conclusion

BlogNest combines the main functions of a blog management system with
AI-assisted content tools. Its backend uses Express.js, MongoDB,
Mongoose, authentication middleware, controllers and AI services to
manage users, blogs, comments and content workflows from one API.

The modular structure provides a foundation for future improvements and
additional content-management or AI capabilities.
